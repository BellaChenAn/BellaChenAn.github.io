Don't listen to the Light: Critical Interaction Design in a Tangible Narrative System

Interactive Digital Narrative (IDN), as a form of expression that blends storytelling with participation, has been widely adopted across many fields. Examples include scenario-based interactive games in theme parks and story-driven dining designs in restaurant settings. In such narrative systems, the audience is not just a task performer, but also an active participant in a branching, multi-ending story experience. In the past, most digital narratives depended on screen-based interaction. However, in recent years, the HCI community has recognized the benefits of physical interaction, leading to the rise of Tangible Narrative (TN). This project proposes and implements an interactive narrative device based on Mixed Reality (MR), which uses critical design to present an art piece. By merging physical interaction with an immersive virtual narrative, this work encourages viewers to reflect on the central themes of its artistic expression.
